Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

This policy describes how the HoneyFruitKids YouTube channel and its related automated tooling handle personal information. HoneyFruitKids is a children's music channel that publishes original nursery rhymes and animated songs for kids.

1. Information We Collect

  • No personal information is collected from viewers or children. The channel does not require accounts, registration, or any personal data to watch its videos.
  • YouTube account data. Our channel is managed through the Google/YouTube account that owns it. The channel's automated upload tooling connects to that single Google account via the YouTube Data API v3 using OAuth 2.0. This connection is used solely to upload videos and manage the metadata (titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails) of our own channel's videos.

2. How We Use the Data

  • Uploading and publishing original music videos on the HoneyFruitKids channel.
  • Setting and editing video titles, descriptions, tags, categories, and custom thumbnails on our own channel.
  • Reading our own channel's video list to avoid duplicate uploads.

3. Data Sharing

Personal data is not sold, traded, or shared with third parties for advertising. The only party that processes the channel's OAuth credentials is Google, as the provider of the YouTube platform and the YouTube Data API. All API access is scoped to the single channel owner's own account and channel.

4. Children's Privacy

Our videos are marked as "made for kids" and are not designed to collect any personal information from children. We do not target ads to or collect data from child viewers. This channel complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

5. Data Security and Retention

API credentials used by the channel's tooling are stored locally on the owner's own computer, encrypted at rest by the operating system. Videos and metadata remain on YouTube's infrastructure and are retained there until deleted by the channel owner.

6. Contact

Questions about this privacy policy can be sent to the channel owner via the contact information listed on the HoneyFruitKids YouTube channel "About" page.
